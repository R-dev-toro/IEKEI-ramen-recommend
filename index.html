First time trying Iekei-style ramen?
Answer four quick questions and we'll find the ideal bowl for you.

Question 1 of 4

How firm do you like your noodles?

Iekei ramen uses thick, flat noodles. Firmness changes how they feel and how well they hold up in the rich broth.

Question 2 of 4

How salty do you like your broth?

The soy-based broth can be dialed up or down. This affects how bold and intense the overall flavor feels.

Question 3 of 4

How much chicken oil do you want?

Iekei broth uses tori-abura (chicken oil) for richness. More oil means a richer, more powerful bowl.

Question 4 of 4

Would you like a side of rice?

Free rice is a beloved Iekei tradition. Pour the leftover broth over it for a soul-warming finish.
